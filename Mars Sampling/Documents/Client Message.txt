Platform: Mobile (phone) is the target. Please build and optimize with mobile performance in mind from the start rather than a PC-scale build. Treat "photo-real" and "kilometre-wide" as a visual direction to aim toward within mobile constraints, not a literal requirement for this milestone - a smaller, well-optimized space that reads as expansive is preferred over a technically-accurate kilometre of terrain that runs poorly on a phone.  Setting & tone: A university student on their first field job, sent out with a colleague ("Good Luck") to collect rock samples, with an absent, unhelpful supervisor ("Big Boss") who's never around to explain anything. Two systems at play - please treat these as related but distinct:  The scientific loop: collect a rock, scan it, get informational feedback (no fail state) on whether it was the right choice. The procedural checklist (bagging, numbering, GPS-verifying, spacing, counting, packaging - listed below): the task structure the tablet walks the player through, wrapping around the scientific loop.  The scanner/sampling judgment mechanic (please implement these specific cases, not random correct/incorrect):  -Bag-fit problem: if a collected rock is too big for the sample bag, the player must decide to get a larger bag or break the rock into smaller pieces -Novelty-bias trap: a shiny/unusual-looking rock should tempt the player, but the "correct" scientific choice is a rock that looks representative of the surrounding samples (average), not the standout - this should be a deliberate, teachable mistake, not arbitrary randomness  Satellite locator step (dialogue-driven, not a map UI): Implement this as a short dialogue exchange with Good Luck rather than a visual map/marker system, e.g.: "What are the coordinates?" / "So we're 5,000km from the last sample?" / "Do you want to check that - it doesn't sound right." The dialogue should let the player catch an error in the reported distance against the required minimum spacing.  In-fiction task checklist (the tablet's targets, wrapping the loop above):  -Confirm you have sample bags, numbered -Use the satellite locator dialogue to log and verify each sample's location and spacing -Enforce a minimum spacing requirement between sample sites via the above -Collect 10 samples + 1 duplicate at the 10th site (11 total) -Lay all 11 out to verify none are missing -Bag and label all 11 for shipment, load onto the vehicle -Send confirmation the shipment is inbound  Scope notes - please read before proposing:  -Environmental hazards (weather events, background aliens as distractions, Good Luck accidentally disturbing samples) are a future-level idea, not required for milestone 1 - do not build these unless we agree to add them. -Given the scope of a test milestone, prioritize a working core loop (movement + sampling + scanner judgment cases + checklist tracking) over visual fidelity or polish.
